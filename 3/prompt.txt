## Goal
When an administrator accepts a signup request, the app must (1) create the Workspace user (already done), (2) once provisioning completes, reset the user's password to a fresh temporary one with change-at-next-login, and (3) email the temporary password to the applicant's verified contact email via Gmail as the acting administrator. This replaces the manual Admin-console handover (open console → Reset password → email → come back and click "Confirm instructions were sent").
